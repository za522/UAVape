Annotation Tool Benchmark for UAVape DCE

Portrait A4 appendix version comparing industry-standard annotation tools against the practical needs of the UAVape Dataset Construction Engine.

primary research computer vision tools appendix table

Purpose. The benchmark asks whether existing annotation platforms can support small-object vape detection workflows: image review, bounding boxes, QA, metadata, YOLO export and iterative model improvement.

Key finding. Mature tools provide strong labelling and export support, but none directly match the full UAVape DCE requirement without configuration or compromise.

Tool Best suited for UAVape fit Main limitation
CVAT Open-source or self-hosted computer-vision annotation. High Robust CV labels and YOLO export. Generic workflow; DCE metadata/provenance needs customisation.
Label Studio Flexible multimodal labelling across CV, NLP, audio and AI evaluation. Medium-high Flexible and open. Configuration-heavy; less CV-specific out of the box.
Roboflow Fast web-based CV dataset annotation, versioning and training export. High Strong YOLO-friendly workflow. SaaS/platform dependence can constrain bespoke research control.
Ultralytics YOLO-native annotation, training, export and deployment workflow. High Excellent for YOLO iteration. Narrower ecosystem focus than a full bespoke curation engine.
Labelbox Enterprise annotation workforces, review pipelines and data operations. Medium Strong QA, heavy for this scale. Enterprise-first cost and complexity.
Encord Enterprise HITL workflows, multimodal data and production AI pipelines. Medium-high Powerful but heavyweight. Commercial platform may exceed dissertation-scale needs.
V7 Darwin Professional image/video/medical annotation with model assistance. Medium-high Strong auto-annotation. More platform-dependent than a custom DCE.
Supervisely Advanced CV workflows, broad modality support and custom apps. High Strong CV and model assistance. Powerful but complex for a targeted lightweight prototype.

Detailed Vertical Benchmark Matrix

Each block keeps the original metric structure while using a portrait layout suitable for A4 export.

Target user / audience
Clarifies whether the tool is designed for researchers, small teams, enterprise labelling teams or full ML operations.
CVAT
Research and engineering teams needing open-source or self-hosted vision annotation.
Label Studio
Flexible open-source labelling for CV, NLP, audio and multimodal AI evaluation.
Roboflow
CV developers wanting dataset annotation, versioning, training and deployment in one web workflow.
Ultralytics
YOLO-focused teams wanting upload, annotate, train, export and deploy in one ecosystem.
Labelbox
Enterprise teams managing large annotation workforces, vendors and review pipelines.
Encord
Enterprise AI teams needing multimodal labelling and production data operations.
V7 Darwin
Professional annotation teams needing image, video or medical annotation workflows.
Supervisely
Enterprise/CV teams needing broad modality support, custom apps and AI tools.
Deployment / access model
Determines whether a tool fits lightweight research, self-hosted privacy needs or paid enterprise deployment.
CVAT
Open-source self-hosted, cloud CVAT Online and enterprise options.
Label Studio
Open-source installable platform plus enterprise HumanSignal version.
Roboflow
SaaS-first web platform with team workspaces and paid credits/features.
Ultralytics
SaaS platform with account regions, cloud training and hosted deployment.
Labelbox
SaaS enterprise platform with external workforces and data-engine workflows.
Encord
Enterprise SaaS/cloud-integrated model; emphasises data staying in the user's cloud.
V7 Darwin
Hosted Darwin platform with CLI, SDK, external storage and workflow integrations.
Supervisely
SaaS, cloud-hosted or self-hosted/on-prem enterprise deployment.
Annotation types
Measures whether the tool supports visual labels needed for object detection and future segmentation or pose expansion.
CVAT
Boxes, polygons, masks, keypoints, skeletons, tracking and 3D point-cloud tasks.
Label Studio
Boxes, polygons, circular regions, keypoints, tracking and semantic segmentation.
Roboflow
Boxes, polygons, masks, keypoints and classification depending on project type.
Ultralytics
Detect boxes, segment polygons, semantic masks, pose keypoints, oriented boxes and classification.
Labelbox
Configurable editors across image, video, text, documents, geospatial, audio and LLM tasks.
Encord
Image, video, audio, text, DICOM, HTML, LiDAR/geospatial and multimodal layouts.
V7 Darwin
Boxes, polygons, masks, brush tools, video, DICOM/NIfTI and medical views.
Supervisely
Images, video, 3D/LiDAR, DICOM, polygons, skeletons, 3D lasso and tracking.
AI-assisted labelling
Important because small-object annotation is labour intensive and benefits from pre-labels, SAM-style tools and model-in-the-loop workflows.
CVAT
Automated annotation using pretrained or custom models via API/SDK; current platform highlights SAM.
Label Studio
Supports ML backends and model pre-labelling, but requires more setup.
Roboflow
Label Assist, Smart Polygon/SAM, Box Prompting and Grounding-DINO-style Auto Label.
Ultralytics
YOLO/SAM smart annotation, including pretrained or own fine-tuned YOLO models.
Labelbox
Model-assisted labelling via Foundry/LLM configuration and imported predictions.
Encord
Strong AI-assisted HITL workflows, SAM 2 support and custom model integration.
V7 Darwin
Auto-Annotate, Auto-Track, SAM and trained-model assisted labelling.
Supervisely
Smart tools, SAM2/ClickSEG-style assistance, built-in models and custom model integration.
Dataset / export fit
Captures whether labels can move cleanly into YOLO training and whether dataset versions are reproducible.
CVAT
Strong export support including YOLO, COCO, Pascal VOC, KITTI, Cityscapes and 20+ formats.
Label Studio
Strong API/SDK flexibility; export/versioning depends on project configuration.
Roboflow
Very strong CV dataset upload, management, versioning, analysis and download.
Ultralytics
Native YOLO dataset structure and numbered NDJSON snapshots for reproducible training.
Labelbox
Strong import/export, schemas, catalogue and API workflows for enterprise pipelines.
Encord
Strong data lineage, cloud storage, APIs and multimodal data management.
V7 Darwin
Strong imports/exports through Darwin CLI/SDK and documented CV export formats.
Supervisely
Strong format coverage, dataset curation, cloud/local storage and API/SDK automation.
Collaboration and QA
Indicates whether the tool can support assignment, review and quality control rather than single-user drawing only.
CVAT
Projects/tasks/jobs, roles, audit trails, reports, validation and review workflows.
Label Studio
Collaborative labelling and review possible; deeper QA is stronger in enterprise setups.
Roboflow
Jobs, assignment, instructions, comments, review mode, approval/rejection and label insights.
Ultralytics
Useful visual controls and class counts, but less mature as workforce QA tooling.
Labelbox
Very strong workflows, issues, comments, benchmarks, consensus and dashboards.
Encord
Role-based workflows, assignment, analytics, custom review and HITL quality controls.
V7 Darwin
Review, consensus, workflow automation, comments and external validation via webhooks.
Supervisely
Review tools, QA statistics, heatmaps, class co-occurrence and balance analytics.
Main limitation
Identifies why an off-the-shelf tool alone may not satisfy the full UAVape DCE requirements.
CVAT
Powerful but generic; candidate review, scrape/synthetic provenance and metadata need customisation.
Label Studio
Flexible but configuration-heavy; not optimised only for YOLO-style small-object CV.
Roboflow
Productive but SaaS/platform-dependent; custom metadata and research control may be constrained.
Ultralytics
Excellent YOLO loop but narrower than a broader bespoke curation engine.
Labelbox
Enterprise-first cost/complexity may exceed a small academic dataset engine.
Encord
Enterprise-first; strong but potentially heavyweight for dissertation-scale pipeline.
V7 Darwin
Strong platform, but less transparent/custom than building a domain-specific DCE.
Supervisely
Broad and powerful but complex; custom DCE may be simpler for targeted vape-litter workflow.
